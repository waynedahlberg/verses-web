Most reading apps are built to keep you moving. Scripture asks for the opposite: a pace slow enough that a sentence can land.

Verses starts from that constraint. A verse is already a complete unit of attention. The product should not bury it under a feed, a streak, or a pile of commentary you did not ask for.

## What we are protecting

- The text, before the chrome around it
- A place you can return to without reconstructing where you left off
- Quiet enough to hear a verse the second time

> The goal is not to help you finish the Bible faster. It is to help you stay with the verse in front of you.

We are building Verses for that kind of reading. If that is the kind you want, you are who this is for.
