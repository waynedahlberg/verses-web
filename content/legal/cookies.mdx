This Cookie Policy explains how the Verses website may use cookies and similar technologies.

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can store on your device. They are commonly used to remember preferences, keep websites functioning properly, or understand how people use a site.

## How Verses Uses Cookies

The Verses website may use a limited number of cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

- remembering basic site preferences
- maintaining essential website functionality
- measuring general website traffic and performance
- helping us understand which pages or features are useful to visitors

Verses does not use cookies to sell personal information or build advertising profiles.

## Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for the website to work correctly. These may support basic functions such as security, preferences, or session-related behavior.

Because these cookies are required for the site to function, they may not always be optional.

## Analytics

Verses may use privacy-conscious analytics tools to understand general website usage, such as page visits, device types, or performance.

If analytics services use cookies or similar technologies, those services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## Third-Party Services

The Verses website may rely on third-party services for hosting, analytics, embedded content, or other website functionality.

Those services may set their own cookies or use similar technologies. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

## Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through browser settings.

Blocking certain cookies may affect how some parts of the website work.

## Changes to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated as the Verses website changes or as new services are added.

The latest version will always be posted on this page.
