Verses is designed to be simple and respectful of your privacy.

## Information We Collect

Verses does not require an account to read or listen to scripture.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or contacts through the app unless you choose to provide information directly to us.

## Reading Data

Reading progress, bookmarks, favorites, goals, streaks, and similar app data may be stored locally on your device.

If Verses later supports cloud synchronization, this policy will be updated to explain what information is stored and how it is handled.

## Analytics and Diagnostics

Verses may use limited diagnostic or analytics information to understand app performance, crashes, and general usage.

Where possible, this information is collected in aggregate and is not intended to personally identify you.

## Audio and Scripture Content

Scripture text and audio playback do not require Verses to collect personal information about what you read or listen to, unless a future feature specifically requires it.

## Notifications

If you enable reading reminders, Verses may schedule notifications on your device. Notification preferences can be changed at any time in the app or in your device settings.

## Third-Party Services

Verses may rely on third-party services for app distribution, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or other infrastructure.

Those services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## TestFlight

Beta versions of Verses may be distributed through Apple TestFlight. Apple may collect information related to beta testing under Apple’s own privacy practices.

## Children’s Privacy

Verses is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Sales

Verses does not sell personal information.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Verses changes. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

## Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent through our [contact page](/contact).
